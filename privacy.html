Privacy policy

Embark: Drive Together, by Shogun Solutions. Last updated 31 October 2026 (voice, shared routes, leaderboards and weather added).

This policy explains what the Embark iPhone app collects, why, and the choices you have. Shogun Solutions is the data controller. Contact us at andynewlands@hotmail.co.uk.

What stays on your iPhone

Your recorded drives (routes, speeds and times), drive stats, saved trips, the places you save privately, and the cars and profile in your garage are stored only on your iPhone unless you choose to share them. We don't receive them.

What we collect when you use an account

  • Account details: your email address and display name, or the name and (possibly hidden) email Apple shares if you use Sign in with Apple. Used to sign you in and show your name to people you drive with.
  • Live location during drives: while you record a drive, your position, speed, display name and current car are sent to members of your groups so they can see you on the map. This is relayed in real time and not stored on our servers.
  • Things you choose to share: group chat messages and photos, locations you share in chat, meets you host and their locations, your RSVPs and the car you're bringing, places you share with a group or everyone, and hazard reports (which disappear after 3 hours). These are stored so the people you shared them with can see them.
  • Public garage: if you turn on "Show my garage to other drivers", your bio, town, cars and car photos are stored and visible to other Embark users. Turning it off deletes them from our servers.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to Embark Pro, Apple handles payment. We receive and store the subscription's transaction ID and expiry date from Apple to confirm your Pro status. We never see your payment details.
  • Meet alerts: if you turn on alerts for meets near you, we store a rough area (rounded to about 1 km) to find meets within 50 miles. Turning alerts off deletes it.
  • Voice: when you talk on a group's walkie-talkie, your voice is relayed live to members of that group. It isn't recorded or stored. If you turn on the "Breaker breaker" summon word, your iPhone listens for it while you're in a voice channel. That speech recognition runs only on your iPhone, and nothing is sent until the word opens the mic.
  • Shared routes: if you share a trip, we store its name, stops and options, and your display name. Anyone with the link can see them. Your own location isn't included, and each driver's route starts from where they are.
  • Leaderboards: we store your monthly miles, number of drives and badges, and show them to members of your groups. We never receive your routes. Turn this off in the Garage tab to delete them.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device's push token to send you chat, meet and RSVP alerts.

We don't use advertising, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and real-time messaging.
  • Apple processes Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and push notifications.
  • LiveKit relays voice between group members in real time.
  • Apple Weather receives the locations of your trip stops to provide forecasts. We don't send Apple your name or account details with them.
  • Apple Maps receives the map areas, searches and route requests needed to show maps and plan routes.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you ask for (contract), and for location sharing and notifications on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. Chat messages and photos stay in their group until your account is deleted. Shared routes and leaderboard totals are kept until your account is deleted. Hazard reports are hidden after 3 hours.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account at any time: Garage tab › Account › Delete account. This deletes your account, messages, photos, shared places, shared routes, leaderboard totals, meets, RSVPs, garage and subscription record from our servers.
  • Stop location sharing by not recording a drive, or by turning off Location for Embark in iOS Settings.
  • Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Embark isn't intended for children under 13.
