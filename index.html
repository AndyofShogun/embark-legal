Embark: Drive Together, by Shogun Solutions

Embark is a UK app for car enthusiasts: plan road trips with turn-by-turn navigation, drive together in groups with live locations and chat, find and host car meets, and keep a garage of your cars.

Common questions

Who can see my location?

Only members of your groups, and only while you're recording a drive. Your live position isn't stored on our servers. Locations you choose to share in a chat, a meet or a shared place are stored so others can see them.

How do I make my garage public or private?

Open the Garage tab and use "Show my garage to other drivers". Turning it off removes your bio, town, cars and car photos from our servers.

How do I cancel Embark Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Embark Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore Embark Pro on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Embark account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Garage tab, scroll to Account and tap Delete account. This removes your account and the data we hold about you. Drives and cars saved only on your iPhone stay there until you delete the app.

Navigation safety

Always follow road signs, the Highway Code and the law. Don't use your phone while driving: set your route before you set off, or use CarPlay.
